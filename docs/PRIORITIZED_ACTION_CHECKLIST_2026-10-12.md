**Status:** Planning / execution tracker — tasks not marked complete unless independently verified  
**Owner:** Brittany ("Aunt B") with Jamie assisting  
**Budget principle:** Free and low-cost steps first; no paid ads until inquiries can be received reliably

> **How to use:** Work from the top. Each checkbox should represent a small, verifiable accomplishment. Mark items complete in GitHub only after they are actually done. Legal/insurance items can run in parallel with website work, but do not treat an unfinished compliance or coverage check as permission to offer a service.

## P0 — Protect incoming leads and clarify what can be offered (do first)

- [ ] **P0.1 — Pick the canonical website.** Confirm which GitHub repository/branch deploys to `auntbs-cleaning-services.netlify.app`, and choose which of the two Netlify sites Brittany will publicly share. **Done when:** one verified working link is chosen.
- [ ] **P0.2 — Test the current website quote form end-to-end.** Submit a harmless test inquiry, confirm it appears in Netlify Forms, verify notification reaches Brittany, and delete test data. Check error state and fallback contact method. **Done when:** an actual submitted inquiry reaches Brittany without guesswork.
- [ ] **P0.3 — Verify direct contact paths.** Test phone, text, email, Facebook Messenger and the website link from a phone or non-admin account. **Done when:** every published contact method works.
- [ ] **P0.4 — Confirm current service scope.** Brittany decides which cleaning, childcare, pet care and house-sitting services she is currently prepared and permitted to provide, what area she will travel to, and any limits. **Done when:** approved list is written down.
- [ ] **P0.5 — Clarify basic business registration/tax obligations.** Check Missouri fictitious-name requirements, local permits and sole-proprietor tax recordkeeping. Use official sources or a qualified local professional. **Done when:** requirements and next actions are identified, with no assumption that operating without an LLC means no obligations.
- [ ] **P0.6 — Request appropriate insurance quotes and verify service coverage.** Ask explicitly about cleaning, childcare, pet care, house sitting, property damage and any future helpers. **Done when:** exclusions and needed coverage are documented. Do not claim insured until coverage is active.
- [ ] **P0.7 — Hold off on staffing promises.** Before paid work by Brittany's sister, clarify worker classification, payroll, wage rules, vehicle use and injury/liability coverage. **Done when:** there is a compliant plan; paying cash alone does not remove these obligations.

- [ ] **P1.4 — Verify Facebook call-to-action and website URL.** Ensure message/contact button and chosen canonical link are visible and work. **Done when:** a test visitor can reach Brittany.

## P2 — Make the website represent the whole business

- [ ] **P2.1 — Update homepage headline and subheading.** Explain cleaning, childcare, pet care and house sitting immediately. **Done when:** mobile visitor understands offerings without scrolling.
- [ ] **P2.2 — Add four primary service cards.** Keep existing cleaning subtypes accessible under cleaning. **Done when:** each category has a clear description and CTA.
- [ ] **P2.3 — Update navigation, footer and metadata.** Replace cleaning-only labels, browser title and search description where appropriate. **Done when:** no misleading cleaning-only wording remains in key conversion surfaces.
- [ ] **P2.4 — Rewrite About section with Brittany's actual story.** Include her longstanding Aunt B nickname and relevant experience, with her approval. **Done when:** personal, accurate, and readable on mobile.
- [ ] **P2.5 — Add service-specific boundaries and FAQs.** Distinguish cleaning, childcare, pet care and house sitting; don't promise dates, insurance or services not confirmed. **Done when:** customer can tell what is and isn't offered.
- [ ] **P2.6 — Test mobile and accessibility.** Navigation, tap targets, text, load performance, focus, labels, error messages. **Done when:** phone-based inquiry is easy and usable.

## P3 — Convert visitors into inquiries

- [ ] **P3.1 — Generalize the website inquiry form.** Ask name, city, one contact method, service, approximate timing and brief description; keep "How did you hear about us?" **Done when:** all four services can be selected without irrelevant mandatory cleaning questions.
- [ ] **P3.2 — Preserve Netlify lead capture.** Keep hidden static form fields aligned with React submission payload; preserve technical `leadSource` and distinct customer-reported attribution. **Done when:** all service types pass a real end-to-end submission test.
- [ ] **P3.3 — Link the existing reusable childcare Google Form.** Confirm parent access without requiring a Google account for ordinary one-session completion, and remove customer-specific schedule wording. **Done when:** phone and desktop test submissions work.
- [ ] **P3.4 — Prepare reusable cleaning, pet-care and house-sitting intake forms.** Keep detailed intake separate from initial lead capture. **Done when:** Brittany has a link for each service she currently offers.
- [ ] **P3.5 — Choose link vs embed for each Google Form.** Test mobile display; offer a direct-link fallback. **Done when:** visitors can complete forms without layout issues.
- [ ] **P3.6 — Protect sensitive information.** Restrict childcare/emergency responses to authorized caregivers, avoid sending details through analytics/lead notification emails, and set retention/access rules. **Done when:** privacy and access approach is documented and tested.
- [ ] **P3.7 — Write two message templates.** One for responding to a new inquiry; one polite follow-up when a prospect goes quiet. **Done when:** Brittany can copy/edit them quickly.

## P4 — Build local trust and discovery

- [ ] **P4.1 — Add authentic owner photo and service images.** Use Brittany's approval and client consent; no identifiable children or private client details. **Done when:** approved images replace placeholders.
- [ ] **P4.2 — Request honest reviews from satisfied customers.** No purchased, fabricated or pressured reviews. **Done when:** requests are sent and genuine reviews displayed with permission.
- [ ] **P4.3 — Check Google Business Profile eligibility.** If eligible, set up an accurate service-area profile with suitable privacy settings; do not create duplicate/fake locations. **Done when:** verified or a reason for deferral is recorded.
- [ ] **P4.4 — Publish one useful post per service category.** Only promote currently available, appropriately covered services. **Done when:** each approved service has a clear, informative post.
- [ ] **P4.5 — Share selectively to local groups.** Follow each group's rules; record which posts drive inquiries. **Done when:** legitimate outreach is done without spam.
- [ ] **P4.6 — Ask permission to place flyers/cards on community boards.** Link QR code to the tested canonical website. **Done when:** placements and source labels are tracked.
- [ ] **P4.7 — Encourage referrals and repeat work.** Follow up with satisfied clients where appropriate and with consent. **Done when:** a simple, repeatable process exists.

## P5 — Measure and improve (weekly)

- [ ] **P5.1 — Create a simple lead tracker.** Fields: date, service, source, area, status, quote, booking, reason lost, next follow-up. No child health or home-access secrets.
- [ ] **P5.2 — Record each new inquiry.** Distinguish technical source (website, Facebook, etc.) from what the customer reports (referral, group, Google).
- [ ] **P5.3 — Review weekly.** Count inquiries, quotes, bookings and reasons people didn't proceed. **Done when:** one weekly review has occurred; repeat thereafter.
- [ ] **P5.4 — Improve the weakest conversion step.** If few inquiries: visibility; if many inquiries but few quotes: responsiveness/qualification; if quotes don't book: scope, pricing, trust or availability. **Done when:** one evidence-based adjustment is made.
- [ ] **P5.5 — Consider paid ads only after funnel works.** Decide budget, objective and stop-loss in advance; no automatic ad spend.
